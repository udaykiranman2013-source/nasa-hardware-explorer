MISSION TIMELINE

From footprints to interstellar space.

01

1960s — First planetary explorers

Early robotic missions began returning close-up measurements and images of other worlds.

02

1969–1972 — Apollo

Astronauts deployed scientific experiments and left hardware on the Moon.

03

1977 — Voyager

Voyager began its journey through the outer solar system.

04

1997 — Sojourner

The first successful Mars rover demonstrated robotic mobility on another planet.

05

2004–2018 — Spirit & Opportunity

Twin rovers investigated Martian geology and evidence of ancient water.

06

2012 — Curiosity

A mobile laboratory began studying the geology and ancient habitability of Gale Crater.

07

2021→ — Perseverance

Perseverance studies ancient environments, collects samples and demonstrates new technologies.

INSIDE THE SPACECRAFT

Instruments turn worlds into data.

📷

Camera

Captures images of landscapes, rocks, clouds and spacecraft surroundings.

🌈

Spectrometer

Studies light to identify materials and investigate chemical composition.

🧲

Magnetometer

Measures magnetic fields around planets and in space.

☢️

Particle Detector

Measures energetic particles travelling through space.

🌡️

Thermal Sensor

Measures temperature and thermal properties of surfaces and environments.

📡

Radio System

Sends commands to spacecraft and transmits scientific data back to Earth.
